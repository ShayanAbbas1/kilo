Bodyweight and timed exercises

- Bodyweight exercises now log as "+kg" (added weight) and count toward
  tonnage, so sets of pull-ups no longer read as no work done. They also
  show up in the progression graph.
- Timed exercises (planks, carries, hangs) can be logged at all now.
- New app icon.
- Violet is the default theme.

Updates now arrive by installing a new release rather than over the air.
Obtainium or your F-Droid client will pick them up automatically.
